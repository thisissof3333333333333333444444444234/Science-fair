The Science Secret

From Zinc to Acid to Copper
The Zinc Nail:
Zinc is a metal that gives away its power easily. When the sour fruit juice touches the zinc nail, a reaction starts. This leaves behind tiny negative particles called electrons.
The Fruit Acid:
The natural juice inside the fruit acts as a helper called an electrolyte. The acid lets electricity move and keeps the reaction going.
The Copper Plate:
Copper pulls electrons toward it. Because the electrons are crowded on the zinc nail, they rush through the wires to get to the copper plate.
The Complete Circuit:
When electrons move from the zinc, through the wires, and into the copper, they form an electric current. This moving line of electrons is what we call electricity.
