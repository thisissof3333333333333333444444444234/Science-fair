📖 How It Works

🔩 Zinc ➡️ ⚡ ➡️ 🍋 Acid ➡️ ⚡ ➡️ 🟤 Copper
🧪 The Electrolyte:
Many fruits contain natural juices like citric acid. This fluid acts as an electrolyte, allowing electricity to travel through it!
🔋 The Electrodes:
We use two special metals: a Zinc nail and a Copper plate. The acidity triggers a reaction that forces electrons to travel from the zinc over to the copper, generating an electric current.
🌍 Why Food Waste?
Instead of putting fruit cores and peels in the trash, we can use their chemical properties to power small devices in an eco-friendly way!
