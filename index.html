📖 How It Works

🔩 Zinc ➡️ ⚡ ➡️ 🍋 Acid ➡️ ⚡ ➡️ 🟤 Copper
🧪 The Electrolyte:
Fruits contain natural acids (like citric acid in lemons)[cite: 9, 16]. This acid acts as an electrolyte, a specialized liquid that allows electricity to flow[cite: 16]!
🔋 The Electrodes:
We use two different metals: a Zinc nail and a Copper plate[cite: 23]. The acid in the fruit triggers a chemical reaction that forces electrons to flow from the zinc to the copper, creating an electrical current[cite: 16, 40].
🌍 Why Food Waste?
Instead of throwing away old fruit skins and cores, we can reuse their stored chemical energy to power small electronics cleanly and safely[cite: 8, 43, 45]!
