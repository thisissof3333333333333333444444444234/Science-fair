📖 How It Works

🔩 Zinc ➡️ ⚡ ➡️ 🍋 Acid ➡️ ⚡ ➡️ 🟤 Copper
🧪 The Electrolyte:
Fruits contain natural acids (like citric acid in lemons). This acid acts as an electrolyte, a magical liquid that allows electricity to flow!
🔋 The Electrodes:
We use two different metals: a Zinc nail and a Copper plate. The acid in the fruit pushes electrons from the zinc to the copper, creating an electrical current.
🌍 Why Food Waste?
Instead of throwing away old fruit skins and cores, we can use their stored chemical energy to power small electronics cleanly and safely!
